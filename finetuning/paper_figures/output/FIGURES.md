## Draft captions

**F01. Cohorts and class balance.** (A) Grade distribution of the clean APTOS splits, the archived Messidor cohort and the verified Messidor-2 cohort (1,744 gradable images, adjudicated grades); shares below 9% are not labelled. (B) Training images per grade per epoch: Qwen draws a class-balanced sample with an inverse-frequency weighted sampler; RetiZero sees every image once, with an inverse-frequency weighted loss and online augmentation.

**F02. Headline performance in-domain and on Messidor-2.** Accuracy, QWK and macro AUC on the 361 clean APTOS test images and on the 1,744 verified Messidor-2 images (trained on APTOS only). Bars: mean over three seeds (one run for zero-shot); dots: individual seeds.

**F03. All paired comparisons.** Paired differences (A − B) with 95% hierarchical bootstrap confidence intervals (images × seeds; exams × seeds on Messidor-2; patients × seeds on archived Messidor). Filled markers: interval excludes zero.

**F04. LoRA rank and alpha grid.** APTOS-361 accuracy and QWK for LoRA rank 8, 16 and 32 with alpha 16, and with alpha = rank, CLAHE on and off. At the validation-selected learning rate (2e-4) every run predicts all five grades and no rank-vs-16 difference is significant; at the original 5e-4 several runs fail (no 5e-4 runs exist for alpha = rank at ranks 8 and 32). Dots: seeds; lines: means.

**F05. Learning rate selection and failed runs.** (A) Validation loss against APTOS test accuracy for every fine-tuned Qwen run; failed runs (at most three grades predicted) have validation losses that do not overlap with working runs (grey band). (B) Mean validation loss by learning rate (rank 16, CLAHE on, three seeds; test accuracy under each bar); the lowest validation loss selects 2e-4 without using the test set.

**F06. With and without the LoRA adapter.** The same 4-bit Qwen3-VL-8B base with no adapter (zero-shot) and with the LoRA adapter at lr 5e-4 and 2e-4, scored with the same five-answer likelihood scorer, on APTOS-361 and verified Messidor-2, CLAHE on and off. Bars: mean; dots: seeds.

**F07. CLAHE on vs off.** Effect of CLAHE on each model, per seed (open: off; filled: on; lines join the means). No fine-tuned model shows a consistent CLAHE benefit; CLAHE lowers zero-shot Qwen accuracy.

**F08. Confusion matrices.** Row-normalised confusion matrices for the headline Qwen model (LoRA r16, lr 2e-4, CLAHE on) and RetiZero (CLAHE off), on APTOS-361 and verified Messidor-2; three seeds pooled; counts in brackets.

**F09. Error distance.** Share of predictions that are exact, or off by one, two, or three or more grades (shares below 7% not labelled). Most errors are between adjacent grades, which is why QWK stays high while accuracy is moderate.

**F10. Class-wise sensitivity and specificity.** One-vs-rest sensitivity and specificity per grade (mean ± SD over three seeds) for Qwen (lr 2e-4, CLAHE on) and RetiZero (CLAHE off).

**F11. One-vs-rest ROC curves per grade.** One-vs-rest ROC curve for each DR grade, computed from each grade's own probability column, for Qwen (lr 2e-4, CLAHE on) and RetiZero (CLAHE off); one line per seed; numbers are the mean per-seed AUC.

**F12. Referable DR: ROC and operating points.** ROC curves for referable DR (grade ≥ 2; score p2 + p3 + p4), one line per seed; dots mark each model's own operating point (predicted grade ≥ 2). On Messidor-2 the fine-tuned models operate at high specificity (about 0.97) and low sensitivity (about 0.3), well below what their ROC curves allow; a recalibrated threshold would be needed for screening.

**F13. Mild vs Normal across cohorts.** Mild (grade 1) eyes predicted Normal, Normal eyes predicted Mild, and the AUC separating Mild from Normal, on APTOS and both Messidor cohorts (bars: mean; dots: seeds; RetiZero with CLAHE was not scored on archived Messidor). Mild-vs-Normal discrimination falls to chance on Messidor; the cause is suspected domain shift, not confirmed by a grader.

**F14. Predicted vs true grade distribution.** Share of images per grade: true labels (black) against each model's predictions (mean over seeds). On Messidor-2 the fine-tuned models predict too few moderate and severe cases, i.e. they under-grade.

**F15. Calibration.** Reliability diagrams (top-probability confidence in 10 bins, seeds pooled, bins with at least 10 images) and expected calibration error per seed. Both models are over-confident on Messidor-2.

**F16. Computational efficiency.** Top: model size, training time for one full run, inference time per image (batch 1, warm median) and peak GPU memory for Qwen3-VL-8B + LoRA r16 (4-bit) and RetiZero ViT-L/16 (all weights trained), measured on the same RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB. Bottom: Qwen inference time and whole-card energy per image for seven configurations, which do not differ.

**F17. Seed-to-seed variability.** Accuracy of every seed of every fine-tuned condition on APTOS-361, sorted by mean; lines span the seeds. Runs at lr 5e-4 vary widely and include failed runs; at lr 2e-4 and for RetiZero the spread is a few points.

**F18. Near-duplicate sensitivity.** Mean accuracy and QWK on all 361 test images and after removing test images with a perceptual-hash near-duplicate in training at distance 0, ≤ 2 and ≤ 4.

**F19. Archived vs verified Messidor.** (A) Accuracy of each run on the archived Messidor inputs against the inputs rebuilt from the raw photos, same 1,057 images: Qwen is identical; RetiZero differs slightly because its archived route went raw → 512 → 224. (B) Archived accuracy against accuracy on the full verified Messidor-2 cohort.

**F20. Qwen training and validation loss by learning rate.** Training loss (A, running median of the trainer log) and validation loss after each epoch (B, log scale) of Qwen rank 16, CLAHE on, at three learning rates, three seeds each. Training stops when the validation loss has not improved for two evaluations, so runs differ in length, and the checkpoint with the lowest validation loss is kept (outlined dot). The failed run (dashed, lr 5e-4) never reaches the validation loss of any working run, as in F05A. The two large spikes (one lr 2e-4 and one lr 5e-4 seed) come after those runs' kept checkpoints. Mean kept validation loss by learning rate is in F05B.

**F21. RetiZero training curves.** RetiZero (all weights trained) per epoch: training loss, validation loss and validation accuracy, three seeds per setting. Training stops after four epochs without a higher validation accuracy (at most 15 epochs) and the epoch with the highest validation accuracy is kept (outlined dot). Validation loss reaches its minimum by epoch 2 to 7 and then rises while training loss keeps falling.

**F22. Validation loss across the LoRA grid at lr 2e-4.** Validation loss after each evaluation for every adapter in the lr 2e-4 grid (rank 8, 16, 32; alpha 16 or alpha = rank; CLAHE on and off; three seeds each; log scale). Every run reaches a validation loss in the same narrow range, consistent with the absence of any significant rank or alpha effect on the test set (F04). Later rises, the largest a rank-16 CLAHE-on seed at epoch 5, do not change the result: each run keeps its lowest-validation-loss checkpoint.
